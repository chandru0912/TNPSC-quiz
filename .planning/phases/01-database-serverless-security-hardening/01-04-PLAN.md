   - Create `adminLoginLimiter = rateLimit({ windowMs: 15 * 60 * 1000, max: Number(process.env.RATE_LIMIT_ADMIN_LOGIN_MAX || 10), standardHeaders: true, legacyHeaders: false, message: { status: 'error', message: 'Too many admin login attempts. Please try again later.' } })`.

4. In `backend/src/routes/student.ts` (per `SEC-04`):
   - Create `studentLoginLimiter` (`windowMs: 15 * 60 * 1000`, `max: Number(process.env.RATE_LIMIT_STUDENT_LOGIN_MAX || 15)`, `standardHeaders: true`, `legacyHeaders: false`) and `studentWriteLimiter` (`windowMs: 15 * 60 * 1000`, `max: Number(process.env.RATE_LIMIT_STUDENT_WRITE_MAX || 10)`, `standardHeaders: true`, `legacyHeaders: false`).

   - Attach `studentWriteLimiter` to `POST /register`, `POST /forgot-password`, and `POST /reset-password`.
5. In `backend/src/routes/aiGeneration.ts` (per `SEC-04`):
   - Create `aiGenerationLimiter` (`windowMs: 60 * 1000`, `max: Number(process.env.RATE_LIMIT_AI_GENERATE_MAX || 20)`, `standardHeaders: true`, `legacyHeaders: false`) and attach it to `POST /generate` (before `requireAdminAuth` or immediately after `requireAdminAuth` — attach before or after consistently; attaching after `requireAdminAuth` or before ensures abusive requests are capped at 429).
