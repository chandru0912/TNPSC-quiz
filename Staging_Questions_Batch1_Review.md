## 5. Sample Question Reviews

### Sample 1: Unit I (General Science - Physics)
* **Lesson:** Laws of Motion (Class 10 Science)
* **Topic:** Apparent Weight & Weightlessness
* **Difficulty:** Medium
* **Question:** When a lift/elevator is falling freely downward under gravity (acceleration a = g), the apparent weight of a person inside the lift becomes:
  * A) Double the actual weight
  * B) Half of the actual weight
  * C) Equal to zero [CORRECT]
  * D) Unchanged
* **Explanation:** During free fall, the downward acceleration of the lift equals g (a = g). The apparent weight R = m(g - a) = m(g - g) = 0. This state is known as weightlessness.
* **Source Reference:** *Samacheer Kalvi Class 10 Science, Lesson 1: Laws of Motion, Section 1.9*

### Sample 2: Unit II (Geography of India - Tamil Nadu)
* **Lesson:** Human Geography of Tamil Nadu (Class 10 Social Science)
* **Topic:** Automobile Hub & Detroit of Asia
* **Difficulty:** Easy
* **Question:** Which city in Tamil Nadu is renowned as the "Detroit of Asia" due to its large automobile and automotive ancillary manufacturing base?
  * A) Coimbatore
  * B) Chennai [CORRECT]
  * C) Madurai
  * D) Tiruchirappalli
* **Explanation:** Chennai accounts for over 60% of India’s automotive exports and houses major global automakers (Hyundai, Ford, BMW, Renault-Nissan), earning it the title "Detroit of Asia".
* **Source Reference:** *Samacheer Kalvi Class 10 Social Science, Geography Lesson 7, Section 7.4*

### Sample 3: Unit III (History & INM - Tamil Nadu Freedom Struggle)
* **Lesson:** Freedom Struggle in Tamil Nadu (Class 10 Social Science)
* **Topic:** Vedaranyam Salt March & Rajaji
* **Difficulty:** Easy
* **Question:** During the Civil Disobedience Movement in 1930, who organized and led the historic Salt Satyagraha march from Tiruchirappalli to Vedaranyam in Tamil Nadu?
  * A) K. Kamaraj
  * B) C. Rajagopalachari (Rajaji) [CORRECT]
  * C) V.O. Chidambaranar
  * D) Subramania Siva
* **Explanation:** C. Rajagopalachari led 100 dedicated volunteers from Tiruchirappalli on 13 April 1930 and reached Vedaranyam on 28 April 1930 to manufacture salt and break the salt law.
* **Source Reference:** *Samacheer Kalvi Class 10 Social Science, History Lesson 9, Section 9.3*

### Sample 4: Unit IV (Indian Polity - Constitutional Remedies)
* **Lesson:** Indian Constitution: Preamble, Rights & DPSP (Class 10 Social Science)
* **Topic:** Fundamental Rights & Article 32
* **Difficulty:** Medium
* **Question:** Which Article of the Constitution of India was characterized by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar as the "Heart and Soul of the Constitution"?
  * A) Article 14
  * B) Article 19
  * C) Article 21
  * D) Article 32 [CORRECT]
* **Explanation:** Article 32 provides the Right to Constitutional Remedies (issuance of writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Quo Warranto, Certiorari) without which fundamental rights are ineffective.
* **Source Reference:** *Samacheer Kalvi Class 10 Social Science, Civics Lesson 1, Section 1.3*

### Sample 5: Unit V (Indian Economy - Welfare Administration)
* **Lesson:** Food Security and Universal PDS in Tamil Nadu (Class 10 Social Science)
* **Topic:** Nutritious Noon Meal Scheme (M.G. Ramachandran)
* **Difficulty:** Easy
* **Question:** In July 1982, Chief Minister M.G. Ramachandran revolutionized child welfare in Tamil Nadu by launching the expanded:
  * A) Nutritious Noon Meal Scheme (சத்துணவுத் திட்டம்) [CORRECT]
  * B) Midday Egg Scheme
  * C) Cradle Baby Scheme
  * D) Kalaignar Insurance Scheme
* **Explanation:** MGR launched the Puratchi Thalaivar MGR Nutritious Meal Programme on 1 July 1982, expanding noon meals to preschool (anganwadi) and school children across the state.
* **Source Reference:** *Samacheer Kalvi Class 10 Social Science, Economics Lesson 3, Section 3.4*
